## Discourse UI icons

`discourse-ui-icons/` contains 277 standalone SVGs generated by [`s/get-discourse-ui-icons`](s/get-discourse-ui-icons) from [`discourse/discourse@a2ec5720ceb745e09ed667c3b40391203f73ca15`](https://github.com/discourse/discourse/tree/a2ec5720ceb745e09ed667c3b40391203f73ca15). The script records the exact upstream inputs and regenerates every tracked icon. It deliberately excludes `discourse-amazon`: the upstream file only identifies SVGRepo as its source, without an asset URL, author, or licence.

The upstream `solid.svg`, `regular.svg`, and `brands.svg` sprites are Font Awesome Free 6.6.0, copyright 2024 Fonticons, Inc., and the extracted SVG icons are CC-BY-4.0. This includes nine Font Awesome-derived symbols stored in Discourse's `discourse-additional.svg`: `discourse-chat-search`, `discourse-bookmark-clock`, `discourse-bell-exclamation`, `discourse-bell-one`, `discourse-bell-slash`, `discourse-table`, `discourse-flask-check`, `table-cells-minus`, and `table-cells-plus`. Font Awesome requires attribution and asks that its embedded attribution comments are not removed. Brand icons remain trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify the represented products or services.

`discourse-additional.svg` also supplies 16 original Discourse symbols, including `bookmark-delete`, `discourse-add-translation`, `discourse-chevron-collapse`, `discourse-chevron-expand`, `discourse-dnd`, `discourse-emojis`, `discourse-h1` through `discourse-h5`, `discourse-other-tab`, `discourse-sidebar`, `discourse-sparkles`, `discourse-text`, and `discourse-threads`. They are recorded as GPL-2.0-only because the pinned Discourse source's [`LICENSE.txt`](https://github.com/discourse/discourse/blob/a2ec5720ceb745e09ed667c3b40391203f73ca15/LICENSE.txt) is GPL version 2.

`discourse-compress` and `discourse-expand` are Font Awesome 4.7.0 glyph outlines, licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1. `tippy-rounded-arrow` derives from Tippy.js 6.3.7 and is MIT-licensed by atomiks.
